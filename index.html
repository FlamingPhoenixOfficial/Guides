GUIDE 01STORAGE SYSTEMS

APPLIED ENERGISTICS 2 · OLDER VERSION

AE2 in Minecraft 1.7.10

Build a computer-like storage network. Put items in one place, then find them from connected terminals.

Version matters. This guide is for the old Minecraft 1.7.10 era of AE2. Recipes and modpack rules vary. Check the recipe in NEI or your pack's own item browser before crafting.
ON THIS PAGEStart hereBuild a small networkStore itemsChannels, simply explainedWhen it does not workQuick word list

01 · FIRST, THE IDEA

What does AE2 do?

Applied Energistics 2 (AE2) moves items through a network. You use a screen called a terminal to see and request those items.

Items→ME network→Terminal

ME means Matter Energy. It is the name used for AE2's storage network. Think of the network as a small connected computer.

02 · A SMALL FIRST BUILD

Build a basic network

Start small. You do not need automation or a giant room of machines.

Checkpoint: If the terminal opens and shows your items, your basic network works.

03 · UNDERSTAND YOUR STORAGE

A cell is not one big box

A storage cell has a total space limit and a limit on how many different item types it can hold. Lots of different items can fill the type limit before the cell looks full.

  • ME Drive: Holds storage cells.
  • Storage cell: Stores items. Read its tooltip for its limits.
  • ME Terminal: Lets you view, put in, and take out stored items.

Keep some cells empty as a backup. If one type of item fills the available space, extra items might not go into that cell.

04 · A COMMON CONFUSION

Channels, simply explained

In many 1.7.10 AE2 versions, a network has a limit on how many devices its cables can support. Each device can use a channel. The ME Controller supplies channels.

Use ordinary ME cable for a small network. Use dense cable when you need more channels on one cable path. The exact numbers depend on the version and pack.

Some packs disable channels or change their rules. If a device says “no channel,” check the pack's AE2 settings and the cable path before rebuilding everything.

05 · QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

The terminal is blank or offline

  1. Check that the network has power.
  2. Check that every block touches a connected cable or network block.
  3. Check that the ME Drive contains a working storage cell.
  4. Look at the device for an error such as “no channel.”
  5. Check the exact item recipe in NEI. The pack may change it.

GUIDE 02STORAGE SYSTEMS

APPLIED ENERGISTICS 2 · LATEST UPSTREAM RELEASE

AE2: current-release guide

The same big idea as older AE2: connect machines to a powered network, then store and find items through a terminal.

Latest upstream release checked 12 October 2026: v26.1.13-beta. A modpack may include a different AE2 version or change its recipes and settings. This guide explains the shared basics; check the version shown in your launcher and use the matching official documentation.
ON THIS PAGEWhat changed?Your first networkCells and item typesAutomatic craftingWhen it does not workQuick word list

01 · THE BIG IDEA

What changed in newer versions?

The basic idea is familiar: connect AE2 blocks, power the network, and use a terminal. Names, recipes, textures, and options can differ from Minecraft 1.7.10.

In some newer setups, channels can be turned off or work differently. Do not assume that an old tutorial's channel count or recipe matches your game.

Before building: Look up the AE2 version in your launcher or mod list. Open the official guide for that version.

02 · A SMALL FIRST BUILD

Make a storage network

Checkpoint: Your test item stays in the list after you close and reopen the terminal.

03 · KEEP ITEMS ORGANIZED

Cells have limits

A cell is limited by the amount of stored data and by the number of different item types. Read the cell tooltip to see its limits.

Use a cell workbench or the pack's cell tool to control which items a cell accepts, if your version includes it. A cell with a filter is useful for items you want to keep separate.

For a safe first setup, use more than one cell rather than trying to fit everything into one.

04 · WHEN STORAGE WORKS

Try automatic crafting later

Automatic crafting tells AE2 how to make an item, then asks the network to make it for you. It is a separate project. Get basic storage working first.

  1. A pattern is a recipe card for the network.
  2. A molecular assembler crafts the recipe.
  3. An interface or pattern provider gives the recipe and ingredients to the crafting setup. The name depends on your version.
  4. Use the crafting terminal to request one test item. Read any missing-item message.

First test a small recipe with ingredients you already have. Do not begin with a large factory.

05 · QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

Check these in order

  1. Does the network have power?
  2. Are the blocks connected with the right cable?
  3. Is there a storage cell in the drive?
  4. Does your version require channels? Read the device's warning.
  5. Does the recipe match this pack's recipe viewer?

GUIDE 03POWER & MACHINES

GREGTECH · STARTER GUIDE

GregTech, one machine at a time

GregTech adds many machines and recipes. The key habit is simple: check the recipe, check the power, then run one test.

There are different GregTech versions. GregTech 5, GregTech Community Edition, GregTech CEu, and pack-specific forks can work differently. The examples below use common GregTech-style ideas. Use your modpack's recipe viewer and quest book as the final answer.
ON THIS PAGEThree useful ideasVoltage and tiersRun a machineCables and powerWhen it does not workQuick word list

01 · THREE USEFUL IDEAS

Start with these

EU is a kind of electricity used by GregTech and some other mods.

Voltage is the power tier a machine can accept. Amperage is how many units of power can travel at once.

A recipe also has requirements. A machine may need the right voltage, enough power, and all the ingredients.

1

Recipe: What goes in, what comes out, and which machine to use.

2

Voltage: Whether the machine can safely accept the power tier.

3

Connection: Whether the cable links the power source to the machine.

02 · POWER TIERS

Voltage rises by tier

In common GregTech 5-style tiers, each step has a higher voltage. These are familiar early tiers, but pack versions and exact rules can differ.

ULVVery early power
LV32 V
MV128 V
HV512 V
EV2,048 V

Important: A voltage that is too high can break a machine in many GregTech versions. Do not guess. Check the machine's tier and the power output before connecting it.

Power needs depend on the recipe. A machine that is fast enough for one recipe may not have enough power for another.

03 · RUN ONE RECIPE

Use this safe routine

Checkpoint: The machine completes one recipe without a warning or damage.

04 · CONNECT THINGS CAREFULLY

Cables carry power

GregTech cables have limits. Their material can set the voltage they can handle, and their size can affect how much power they carry and how much is lost.

  • Read the cable's tooltip before placing it.
  • Use a cable rated for the machine's voltage.
  • Keep cables short when you can. Some cables lose power over distance.
  • Leave some space between power sources and machines until you understand their connection rules.

Different machines can use different sides for power. Check the machine face or its instructions; do not assume every side works.

05 · QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING

The machine will not start

  1. No power? Check the generator, cable connection, and machine input side.
  2. Wrong tier? Check that the voltage matches what the machine can accept.
  3. Recipe will not start? Compare every ingredient and the selected recipe.
  4. Not enough power? Add power generation or try a less demanding recipe.
  5. Still stuck? Read the quest book and search the exact error in your pack's wiki.

06 · KEEP THESE WORDS HANDY

Quick word list

GregTech's electricity system.
Voltage
The strength or tier of the electrical input.
Amperage
How many units of power are sent together.
Tier
A level. Higher tiers usually need stronger materials and machines.
Recipe viewer
An in-game tool that shows how to make items in your pack.

GUIDE 04THE WHOLE MODPACK

GT NEW HORIZONS · A GENTLE START

Your first steps in GTNH

GT New Horizons is a long progression modpack for Minecraft 1.7.10. The quest book helps you learn one stage at a time.

Use the quest book first. GTNH is actively updated. Recipes and progression change. This is a calm orientation guide, not a replacement for your installed version's quests and the official wiki.
ON THIS PAGEWhat is GTNH?First dayFollow the questsThe big progression mapSurvival remindersWhere to find helpQuick word list

01 · WHAT YOU ARE PLAYING

A long game with a guidebook

GTNH combines technology, exploration, magic, and survival. GregTech machines and voltage tiers shape much of the technology progression.

It is designed to take a long time. You do not need to understand the whole pack today. The in-game quest book has thousands of quests, explains many tasks, and can show item recipes when you click items.

02 · YOUR FIRST PLAY SESSION

Make the first day manageable

Checkpoint: You have a safe place, light, food, and a clear next quest. That is enough for now.

03 · YOUR IN-GAME HELPER

Let the quest book lead

When a quest asks for an item, click the item in the quest. In many GTNH versions, this opens the recipe in NEI, the in-game recipe browser.

  1. Read the quest's full description.
  2. Click an item to see its recipe.
  3. Make one required item at a time.
  4. Return to the quest and check the requirement.
  5. If the recipe does not match, trust the game you installed and check the official wiki.

It is normal to have several open goals. Pick one small task and finish it before starting a new machine line.

04 · THE BIG PICTURE

Progress happens in stages

The official quest categories show this broad path. Names or order may differ slightly with pack releases.

Stone age

Learn basic tools and survival.

Steam

Build early machines and learn to supply them.

LV, then MV and HV

Begin electricity. Learn recipes, voltage, cables, and machine tiers.

EV and beyond

Unlock more machines, processing, automation, and new goals.

Exploration and late game

Later quests can send you to other dimensions and planets.

Some quests cover food, building, machines, power, magic, and exploration at the same time. You do not have to finish every side quest before you can take the next step.

05 · SMALL THINGS THAT HELP

Make survival easier

  • Darkness: Place lights near your base before night.
  • Food: Eat different kinds of food. Repeating one food is less helpful in this pack.
  • Mining: Ore appears in large veins. Explore carefully and mark places you want to find again.
  • Power: Check voltage before connecting a machine. Too much voltage can damage it.
  • Materials: Store spare parts and label chests. Complex recipes use many steps.
  • Rest: Save a note about your current goal. You can stop and return later.
